Analog Drift

A model of how real analog oscillators wander in pitch: slow 1/f drift, thermal random walk, fast FM jitter, warm-up, and per-voice tolerance. Hold the chord and flip between Clean and Drift on headphones. Then watch what each voice is doing.

Audio off

Five sawtooth voices (C3 E3 G3 B3 D4) through a gentle lowpass, each with its own drift. Clean sets the drift depth to zero, so the pitch is exact. Restart voices re-seeds every voice and replays the warm-up.
